Welcome to my personal portfolio! This website showcases my projects, skills, and experiences, designed to give you a glimpse into who I am and what I do.

## Technologies Used

- **Quartz Framework**: A minimalist static site generator for building fast, flexible websites using Markdown.
- **Markdown**: Used to create and format the pages of the website.
- **Vercel**: Deployed using Vercel, ensuring fast and reliable hosting for my portfolio.
- **Figma**: Used for designing the layout and overall user experience of the website.
- **REST API**: Integrated to fetch and display my latest YouTube video.

## Features

- **Year**: A timeline of my milestones and accomplishments.
- **Projects**: A collection of my work, from coding projects to personal creations.
- **Contact**: Easy way to get in touch with me.
- **About**: Learn more about my background, experience, and what I’m passionate about.
- **Latest YouTube Video**: Automatically fetches and displays my latest YouTube video using a REST API.
